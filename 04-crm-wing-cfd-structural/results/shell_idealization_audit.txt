2. Converter (scripts/ucrm_to_ccx.py) constitutive conversion - verified

5. Status
   - Static tip vs NASTRAN 2.5239 m: CalculiX -7.4% (tolerance 2%): MISSED.
   - Modal first 5 modes within 2.83% (tolerance 5%): PASSED.
   - CalculiX modes 6, 8, 9 (7.863, 9.845, 10.007 Hz) are local to PIDs 94 and 95 (single ribs); hypothesis: artifact of the
     soft rib webs. Tested in section 6 (rib x10 probe).
   - Shell idealization: layered-shell route investigated (section 7) and stopped by decision D10 (2026-10-22);

6. Rib probe (scripts/variant_rib_scale.py; decks simulation/modal_v1/coarse_rib10, not a model change)
   - All elastic constants of materials M94 and M95 (the two root ribs) multiplied by 10; Poisson ratios unchanged.
   - Baseline F2 f [Hz]: 1.675, 2.582, 3.867, 4.760, 6.338, 7.863, 8.251, 9.845, 10.007, 10.674.
   - Probe f [Hz]: 1.677, 2.583, 3.872, 4.764, 6.339, 8.256, 10.683, 11.402, 12.788, 13.556.
   - Baseline modes 6, 8, 9 (7.863, 9.845, 10.007 Hz) are absent from the probe; the other baseline modes shift by <1%.
   - Probe vs NASTRAN coarse, modes 1-7: +2.93, -1.84, +1.46, +1.72, +0.38, +1.77, +0.84 %.
   - Probe mode 8 (11.402 Hz) has no NASTRAN partner; modes 9-10 (12.788, 13.556) are near NASTRAN 12.843, 13.975
     but are not shape-verified (only NASTRAN frequencies are available).
   - Conclusion: modes 6, 8, 9 of the baseline are rib-web panel modes of PIDs 94 and 95. The pre-registered modal
     criterion (first 5 modes within 5 %) holds in both baseline (2.83 %) and probe (2.93 %).
   - Mode 1 f^2 ratio 1.059 (about 6 % too stiff); mode 2 f^2 ratio 0.963 (about 3.7 % too soft): signs are mixed.

7. Layered-shell investigation (decisions D9, D10)
   Scripts: layered_fit.py, layered_abd_export.py, strip_test.py. Results: results/layered_fit_*.txt, results/layered_abd_export_coarse.txt
   - Fit (symmetric stack of N equal plies at 0 deg, per-component bounded least squares of A, B, D, ply stiffness floor 1e-3,
     H scanned from T to 25 T): the diagonal components 11, 22, 66 are exact for all 242 properties (N = 25, H/T <= 9.97).
     Component 12 (A12, B12, D12) is exact for 112/242 properties = 59.2 % of elements. N = 25 and N = 50 give identical
     results; floor 1e-4 raises the exact share to 65.9 % of elements. Ply positive definiteness (|Q12| <= sqrt(Q11 Q22)) limits A12:
     for PID 94 components 11, 22, 66 pass together only for H/T in [10.0, 12.1], component 12 needs H/T >= 18.0.
   - Best-effort fit (npz): effective membrane moduli of the non-exact 40.8 % of elements are off by up to 6.97 % (p90 6.5 %);
     ply stiffness contrast median 24.5, max 1.98e4.
   - CalculiX 2.23: *SHELL SECTION COMPOSITE is allowed only for S8R or S6 shell elements (log message); an S8R strip with 25 plies has
     8473 equations for 1213 nodes (same count as the homogeneous strip).
   - Strip verification (property 1, material axis 1, 3 m x 0.3 m cantilever, tip load 100 N); ratio = CalculiX tip uz / laminate theory:
       homogeneous S4  axis 1 0.9945, axis 2 0.9961;  homogeneous S8R 0.9940
       composite, 25 identical plies 0.9955;  composite, 1 ply 0.9956
       composite, symmetric fitted stack (B = 0) 0.8352;  composite, asymmetric fitted stack 3.9775
   - Conclusion: CalculiX composite handles thick uniform stacks but does not reproduce laminate theory for stacks with strong
     through-thickness stiffness variation (symmetric: 16.5 % too stiff; asymmetric: 4.0x too flexible). The cause was not identified
     (untested hypothesis: too few through-thickness integration points to resolve the stack).
   - Decision D10: layered route stopped; orthotropic S4 shell kept; static tip miss (-7.4 % vs 2 % tolerance) documented as a limitation.

9. FORCE BALANCE OF THE STATIC DECK (uCRM-9 coarse, static_v3/coarse_tr_f2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Script: scripts/check_equilibrium.py     Output: results/static_equilibrium_coarse.txt
- Printed RF at the print set NSPC is K*u = support reaction + external load at those nodes
  (CLOAD, shell gravity, point-mass weight transferred through *EQUATION). The reaction is therefore
  printed total - (CLOAD + transferred gravity) on all DOFs of the NSPC nodes.
- Shell mass in the deck 11,958.49 kg = spreadsheet 11,958.5 kg (ratio 1.0000). Point masses (T3D2 pairs) 22,170.00 kg.
- CLOAD sum Fz +967.01 kN; gravity sum Fz -334.80 kN (of which -21.59 kN on NSPC nodes); net 632.21 kN.
- Reaction at NSPC Fz = -632.21 kN. Closure R + A + W: Fx -7.7e-5 N, Fy +4.4e-3 N, Fz -2.5 N (0.0004 % of the net load).
- The default-mode deck (coarse_trall, E = 1 Pa) gives identical balance and displacements.
- Consequence: the load carried by the CalculiX deck equals the net load of the NASTRAN reference and the shell mass matches.
  The static tip gap (mean -7.41 %, maximum -6.32 % vs 2.5239 m) is therefore a stiffness difference, not a load or mass
  discrepancy. It remains a documented limitation (D10).
- Corrections: an earlier internal estimate of a ~6 % shell-mass gap, a 14.2 kN weight on the supports, and a
  load-normalised stiffness error of about -8.5 % were wrong. They came from post_static.py's equilibrium block,
  which ignored gravity on the NSPC nodes. That block is removed; check_equilibrium.py replaces it.
