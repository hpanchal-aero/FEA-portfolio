WING-ALONE SURFACE FROM THE uCRM-9 'wbt' GEOMETRY (decision D11, option A)
Scripts: wing_edge_matching.py, sew_test.py, build_wing_geometry.py. Records: wing_edge_matching.txt, wing_sewing_test.txt, wing_geometry_build.txt.

Faces kept (original tags of uCRM_9_wbt.igs): 5, 26 (inboard wing, y = 0 to 3.05 m), 11, 12 (exposed wing, inboard), 16, 18 (exposed wing,
outboard), 14, 20 (blunt trailing-edge strips), 22, 24, 27 (tip). Face 30 (junction section at y = 3.05 m) duplicates the junction
edges of faces 5, 12, 11, 26 and is not part of the skin. Remaining faces (fuselage, fairings, tail) are dropped.

Seams (exact polyline distances between boundary curves): leading edge, kink and tip seams coincide to 0.0001-0.022 mm; the
trailing-edge strips sit 0.75 mm (inboard) and 1.20-1.22 mm (outboard) from the skin trailing edges. The sewing tolerance must
therefore exceed 1.22 mm. Sewing at import (Geometry.OCCSewFaces, 1.5 mm): all wing seams are joined; at 0.5 mm the trailing-edge
strip seams stay open (control); 2.5 mm gives the same wing topology as 1.5 mm. Sewing renumbers the faces; they are mapped back
to their original tags by bounding box.

The dataset has no closing face for the blunt trailing edge of the inboard wing (13 mm thick at y = 3.05 m, 17 mm at y = 0).
A ruled strip (0.0466 m2) closes it. Resulting surface: 12 faces, 25 curves, no edge shared by 3 or more faces, 3 free edges that
form one closed loop on the symmetry plane y = 0 (root lower 14.005 m, root upper 13.823 m, strip end 0.017 m).
Area 424.514 m2 (CAD kernel), 424.420 m2 (50 mm surface mesh). Bounding box x 23.064-47.866, y 0-29.488, z 2.419-4.924 m.
The geometry file is kept in the model units of the IGES import (metres x 25.4); scaling to metres is applied at mesh export.

Open item: after sewing, the CAD-kernel area of tip face 27 reads 0 (0.001 m2 before sewing); the surface mesh gives 0.0004 m2 and the
face is topologically closed (three neighbours, no free edges). Cause not established; the face is about 1e-6 of the surface.
Third-party geometry and derived BREP files are not committed (geometry/wing_alone* is ignored).
